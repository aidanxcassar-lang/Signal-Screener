Common questions

How the scoring works

How is the score calculated?

Quality weights adoption and liquidity at 40%, tokenomics at 35%, and scale at 25%. Every figure comes from live public APIs and is reconciled by taking the median across up to four independent sources, so one bad feed cannot move a score on its own.

What does coverage mean?

Coverage is the share of a score's inputs that resolved from real data. Where something cannot be verified it is excluded rather than estimated, and we say what was missing. A high score at low coverage is not a strong result, which is why the figure sits next to every score.

Where does the data come from?

Market data comes from CoinGecko, CoinPaprika, CryptoCompare and Binance, reconciled by median. Protocol revenue and audit records come from DefiLlama, developer activity from GitHub, and holder concentration from Ethplorer. Every source is named on the report it appears in.

Is this financial advice?

No. These are mechanical scores produced by a published framework applied to public data. They take no account of your circumstances and are not a recommendation. Crypto is volatile and you can lose everything you put in.

Why do some assets show no developer or team data?

Because no free public source publishes it reliably. Rather than guess a number, we exclude it and say so on the page. That is the whole reason coverage is shown at all.

Do I need an account or an API key?

Neither. Scoring is free and unlimited, runs entirely in your browser, and stores nothing on our servers. An API key is optional and only unlocks the AI-researched metrics that no public API publishes.
